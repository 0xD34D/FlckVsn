Do you see what they see?  FlckVsn loads the same computer vision models used by Flock ALPR and feeds frames from your camera in realtime.

## How It Works

Just point your camera, pull up the bottom HUD drawer, and pick a model. Go with **pico3** to see what actual Flock cameras see, switch to **nano2** for a finer vehicle breakdown, or crank it up to **large** if you want long-range detections and a temporary battery heater. Filter out the noise by toggling whichever labels you care about, sit back, and watch the HUD track everything in real time.
